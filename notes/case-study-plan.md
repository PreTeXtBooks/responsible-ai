## Sources collected for the ChatGPT workers story, by use

Read in November 2026:

- Guardian, 2 August 2023 (pasted by Lily): the four petitioners (Mophat Okinyi,
  Alex Kairu, Richard Mathenge and one more petitioner who is not named in
  the part we have); 51 moderators on the OpenAI account; Foxglove's Cori
  Crider; the union formed in May 2023; the Kenyan labour court ruling that Meta
  is the "true employer" of its moderators (stated as "last month"); the claim
  that data labelling is done in east Africa, India, the Philippines and in
  refugee camps including Dadaab (Kenya).
- Wall Street Journal, The Journal, 11 July 2023 (pasted by Lily): Alex, Bill
  and Mophat in their own words; why Kenya was chosen (low wages, high
  unemployment, English speakers, good internet); the history of chatbots
  "going off the rails" (Microsoft Tay 2016, Lee Luda 2021, Meta BlenderBot 3
  2022); the Swahili point.
- Australian Human Rights Commission opinion piece, 13 February 2023
  (Finlay, Hooton and Wallace, first published in the Australian Financial
  Review): argues that labour rights are human rights, calls for "danger money"
  and support processes, and notes OpenAI's reported $29bn valuation and
  Microsoft's $10bn investment.
- BBC News video, "AI and data labelling: 'I felt like my life ended'": only the
  blurb was seen. Mophat talks about his personal life.
- Reddit thread on r/todayilearned: not read (Reddit would not load).
