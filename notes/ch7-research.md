## The af.net "Responsible AI Framework" article: NOT used

The pasted article (af.net, "Published Sep 26, 2026", credited to Daily Nation) says Kenya
launched a Responsible AI Framework with the African Union on 26 September 2026,
covering agriculture and finance. I could not find it anywhere else: a search found no Daily
Nation or African Union coverage. It reads like an automatically written summary (it has a "Why
it matters" box and a long unsigned opinion section with predictions).
What the search did find instead, on 22 to 24 September 2026, was a Kenya and
Anthropic Joint Declaration signed in New York for cooperation on responsible AI (The Star,
Capital FM, KBC, others). That's a partnership, not a national framework. One of those
reports also says Anthropic's threat report named a Kenyan actor using Claude for political
posts ahead of the 2027 election. That is a single source and is not used.
Do not use the af.net article unless you can find the original.
